## Required invariants

1. Counting must complete before any replacement mutation.
2. More than `10,000` matches leaves the document unchanged.
3. Replacement slices use at most `8 ms` or `256` items and schedule continuation through `QTimer.singleShot(0, ...)`.
4. The user cannot edit or switch tabs during the operation; the adapter can still perform its own programmatic mutation.
5. Cancel and ordinary failure roll back the single undo transaction; rollback failure must be reported as a possible partial change.
6. A stale callback cannot update a removed/changed tab.
7. The synchronous editor method remains available and drains the same session.
8. The measurement report must not turn the 1.2 MB single-machine sample into a large-file support claim.
