## Decision

**D73-AC05 — accepted-with-limits.** The measured limits are explicit enough
to publish the current development-stage boundary, while the unrun scope stays
open and release-blocking.

The current default application may state that the recorded workloads complete
on this Windows 11 x64 machine under Qt offscreen, including the measured
Unicode/newline/long-line round trips, cancellable Replace All rollback,
Replace All limit safety, stale recovery discard, bounded 64-chunk/1 MiB
recovery handoff, and injected writer-failure cleanup. The handoff bound is not
a product native-memory ceiling.

The project must not state a general large-file range, performance SLA,
clean-machine or cross-machine guarantee, interactive non-offscreen guarantee,
permission/disk-full behavior, hard-power durability, multi-window contention
behavior, or enterprise release readiness. Signing, installer/update, and legal
clearance remain open. Support ownership and the local issue route are now
assigned by the Architect under the team operating model, but that assignment
does not make the candidate releasable.
