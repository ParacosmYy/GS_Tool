# D8.4 Parent Review

- Date: 2026-08-09
- Delivery: D8.4 acceptance evidence traceability preflight
- Owner: Architect
- Disposition: ACCEPTED WITH LIMITS

## Team decision

QA identified that the configurable acceptance contract could contain
evidence-backed statuses with empty evidence, or file-shaped evidence pointing
to missing artifacts, while `scripts/check.ps1` still passed. Project
Management and Product agreed that this was a release-governance gap rather
than a new editor feature. The Architect scoped a small D8.4 preflight and
kept legal, clean-machine, permission-pressure, and other environment gates
independent.

## Implementation

`scripts/check.ps1` now validates `docs/agent-team/acceptance.json` by:

- checking separate subdelivery/scenario status vocabularies;
- requiring non-empty required-evidence entries;
- requiring evidence for `accepted-with-limits`, `completed`,
  `code_verified`, and `smoke_verified` items;
- requiring limits/unrun reasons for `defined`, `environment_pending`, and
  `baseline_only` items; and
- checking explicit repository-relative evidence paths for existence.

The contract records D84-AC01 and D8.4, with ADR-0027 defining the boundary.

## Evidence

- `scripts/check.ps1` PASS after the acceptance matrix, delivery register, ADR,
  and review were added.
- The preflight catches missing evidence and missing limits during development;
  those failures were corrected before the final PASS.
- The existing direct-entrypoint diagnostic was rerun and recorded as S5
  evidence.

## Limits

This is structural traceability, not proof that a descriptive smoke label ran.
It does not authorize permission/disk-pressure experiments, prove clean
Windows x64 startup, establish cross-machine or hard-power durability, or
provide legal, signing, installer, update, or final support approval.
