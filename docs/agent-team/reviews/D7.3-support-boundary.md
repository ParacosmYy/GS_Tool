# D7.3 support-boundary review

## Review purpose

This record is the Architect's integrated disposition of the D7.3 evidence
available in the supplied local checkout. It is a support-boundary decision,
not a certification or a large-file compatibility claim.

## Evidence reviewed

- `docs/performance/baseline-2026-08-09.json` — current source Python + Qt
  offscreen baseline, including the workload matrix, safety probes, event-loop
  probe, native process-memory sample, and injected writer-failure probe.
- `docs/performance/repeat-2026-08-09.json` — three source/offscreen runs with
  safety invariants required by `scripts/repeat_measure.ps1`.
- `docs/performance/packaged-capture-2026-08-09.json` — three runs of the root
  packaged EXE through `--diagnose-capture`, bound to
  `dist/QuillForge.release.json` by `scripts/measure_packaged.ps1`.
- `docs/agent-team/acceptance.json` — D73-AC01 through D73-AC05 criteria.
- `docs/agent-team/reviews/D7.3-qa-disposition.md` — QA interpretation and
  unrun scope.
- `docs/agent-team/reviews/D7.3-product-confirmation.md` — Product scope
  confirmation.

## Accepted-with-limits outcomes

1. The measured source/offscreen matrix covers UTF-8 ASCII LF, CRLF, Unicode,
   and long-line workloads up to the recorded sizes, with round-trip results.
2. The source/offscreen safety probes show that a cooperative Replace All can
   roll back after mutation, a match-limit result leaves text unchanged, and a
   stale recovery capture is discarded after a content-version change.
3. The packaged diagnostic proves the production adapter capture path can
   complete the recorded synthetic workload in the bundled EXE with Qt
   offscreen on this Windows machine.
4. An injected mid-stream writer failure preserves the prior snapshot, removes
   temporary output, and leaves the dirty document eligible for a later retry.
5. The default recovery composition keeps the producer/worker handoff within
   the measured 64-chunk/1 MiB queue bound and completes the atomic commit and
   cleanup path in source/offscreen and packaged-EXE diagnostics.

These outcomes support D73-AC01, D73-AC02, D73-AC03, and D73-AC05 as
`accepted-with-limits`. The writer-failure result is useful D73-AC04 evidence,
but it is not permission or disk-full evidence.

## Explicit support boundary

QuillForge must not publish a supported file-size range, native-memory ceiling,
clean-machine guarantee, or recovery durability guarantee based on this
evidence. The current evidence does not cover:

- clean interactive Windows startup or another machine;
- non-offscreen packaged capture;
- permission denial, disk-full pressure, or hard-power loss;
- multi-window contention or files larger than the recorded workloads;
- a product-wide native-memory ceiling under larger or filesystem-pressure
  workloads.

An isolated temporary ACL experiment was stopped and discarded because the
directory-level denial did not produce a bounded failure in this Windows
environment, while a target-file denial was bypassed by the atomic replacement
operation. It is not accepted as D73-AC04 evidence; no permission or disk-full
support claim is made.

The Architect keeps D7 in progress while D7.2 is accepted with explicit limits. D73-AC04 remains open until an
authorized, safe filesystem-pressure scenario is available, and D73-AC05
is accepted with limits after the Product confirmation joins the QA disposition
in `docs/agent-team/reviews/D7.3-qa-disposition.md`.

## Decision

No large-file support claim is approved. The evidence is sufficient to advance
the measured-workload and packaged-diagnostic slices, while the open limits
remain release-blocking for any broader enterprise support statement.
